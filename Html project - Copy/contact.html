Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

Do you offer catering services?

Yes, we offer full-service catering for events of all sizes. From corporate lunches to weddings and special celebrations, our team can create a custom menu to suit your needs. Please contact us at least 48 hours in advance to discuss your requirements.

How can I make a reservation?

Reservations can be made online through our website, by phone, or using our mobile app. For large groups (8 or more), we recommend calling us directly to ensure we can accommodate your party. Reservations are recommended, especially during peak hours and weekends.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely! We offer a variety of options for different dietary needs, including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and dairy-free choices. Our menu clearly marks these items, and our staff is trained to handle allergy concerns. Please inform your server about any specific dietary requirements.

What is your delivery radius?

We deliver within a 5-mile radius of each restaurant location. Delivery times typically range from 30-45 minutes, depending on distance and current order volume. A delivery fee applies based on distance, and you can track your order in real-time through our mobile app.

Do you have a loyalty program?

Yes, we have the FreshBite Rewards program! Earn points on every purchase, receive birthday rewards, exclusive offers, and more. You can sign up through our website or mobile app. Members also get early access to seasonal menu items and special events.
