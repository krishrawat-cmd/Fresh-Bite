Our Story

How FreshBite became a beloved food chain

FreshBite was founded in 2025 by Krish Rawat with a simple yet powerful vision: to create a restaurant that serves delicious food made from fresh, locally-sourced ingredients without compromising on taste or nutrition.

What began as a single location in downtown New York quickly gained popularity for its innovative menu that combined culinary excellence with nutritional science. Our commitment to quality and sustainability resonated with customers who were looking for healthier dining options without sacrificing flavor.

Over the years, we've grown from that single location to over 50 outlets across the country, but our core values remain unchanged. We still work directly with local farmers, use sustainable practices, and create each dish with the same passion and attention to detail that defined our very first restaurant.
